How to Use

Naming your emotions and associating them with the right reactions in your body is the first step in learning emotional self-regulation. Okay, that was too much :)


Click and rotate the wheel freely to align emotions to the right side for easy reading. Eventually, you'll be able to name what you're feeling. Naming the emotion reduces the intensity of the emotion itself.


Keep a tab open while you're working and try learning to name and note down your emotions. All data is saved locally on your device.

Emotion categories and structure based on work by Geoffrey Roberts
