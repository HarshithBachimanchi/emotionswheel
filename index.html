About This Tool

Welcome to the feelings wheel :) This is a simple html application to learn to name your feelings, and if you'd like to, track your feelings over the day. Click on the feeling you're feeling and it will logged with its main category and sub categories at the bottom. It is primarily to learn to name your feelings without getting overwhelmed (that's also a feeling :)) with bodily reactions.
